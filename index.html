Wrist exercise summary

How these measures are computed

Score: balance (harmonic mean) of path covered and ink kept on the path, reduced when the drawn line is much longer than the guide. Scribbling everywhere scores near zero. Steadiness: derived from the RMS of high-frequency (above ~4 Hz) phone orientation changes; higher means less shake. Smoothness: log dimensionless jerk of each stroke, rescaled to 0–100. Range used: 5th–95th percentile span of phone orientation while tracing, in degrees.

These are exercise measures from consumer phone sensors. They have not been clinically validated and must not be used for diagnosis.
