# Customer discovery interview guide

Who: physical therapists, occupational therapists and hand therapists (aim for 5–10), plus 3–5 people doing home exercises after an injury or stroke, or their caregivers. Possible contacts: Penn State Kinesiology faculty, Mount Nittany Health rehabilitation, campus physical therapy clinics.

Length: 20 minutes. Record notes, not names. Do not demo until the end.

## For therapists

1. Walk me through how you assign home exercises for the wrist and hand today.
2. How do you know whether a patient actually did them? What do you see at the next visit?
3. What fraction of your patients do their home program as prescribed? What gets in the way?
4. Which measurements do you take in clinic for wrist control, tremor or coordination? Which would you want between visits?
5. What would make a home tool trustworthy enough for you to look at its numbers?
6. How would you want to receive results: a printed report, a file, a portal, nothing?
7. Who pays for tools like this in your setting, if anyone?
8. (After demo) What would you change first? Who should we talk to next?

## For patients or caregivers

1. What exercises were you given, and how often do you do them?
2. When do you skip them, and why?
3. What would make you want to do them every day?
4. Would you play with family or friends if it were a game?
5. (After demo) Was anything hard to see, hold or understand?

## After each interview

Write down: the strongest pain point heard, any number they gave (adherence, time, cost), one feature request and one reason they would not use it.
