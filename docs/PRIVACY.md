# Data, privacy and consent

Tilt Sketch works fully on the phone. Two optional features send data to the project's database (Supabase), and both are off until the person turns them on.

| Feature | What is sent | What is never sent | Who can read it | How to stop |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Help train the AI** (home screen, or the prompt after a challenge) | The lines of a free drawing (points scaled 0–1000), the object it was meant to be, Look/Tilt mode, the AI's guess and score, a random phone ID | Name, photos, camera images, location, practice results | Only the project's maintainers, through the secret key used for training | Turn sharing off; **Delete the drawings I shared** removes every drawing from that phone |
| **Feedback** (survey after a session or result, or Send feedback) | Answers from 1 to 5, an optional comment, where it was asked (practice, game…), language, a random phone ID | Name, drawings, results | Only the project's maintainers | Tap Not now; nothing is sent unless the person taps Send |
| **Share with my clinician** (Practice) | For one profile: the name the person types for their clinician, session summaries (scores, path coverage, steadiness, smoothness, speed, range of motion) and the calibrated range, plus a random profile ID | Drawings, other profiles, camera images, location | Only someone with the clinic code **and** its private key | **Stop sharing** deletes that profile's data from the clinic; the clinician can also remove a patient |

## How it is enforced

- Tables have row level security and no direct access for the public key. Every action goes through database functions (`docs/supabase.sql`) that check their input: known objects only, size limits, daily limits, and a matching clinic key.
- The clinic's private key is generated by the database and stored only as a SHA-256 hash. It is shown once to the clinician and cannot be recovered.
- Phone and profile IDs are random and not linked to any account. Patients choose the name their clinician sees.
- Drawings that score very low (likely scribbles) are left out of training (`training/fetch_donations.py --min-score`).

## Limits

- No accounts: whoever holds a clinic's code and key can open it, so clinicians should keep the key like a password.
- Scores are computed on the phone. They are wellness measures from motion sensors, not a clinical assessment, and Tilt Sketch is not a medical device.
- For use in a clinic, a later version would add clinician accounts, a data processing agreement and a HIPAA-ready host. These are on the roadmap, not in the prototype.
