Tilt Sketch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026. This policy covers the Tilt Sketch website (ser2104.github.io/tilt-sketch) and the Tilt Sketch Android app.

The short version

Tilt Sketch has no accounts and no ads, and it does not sell or share data with advertisers. The AI that judges drawings runs on your phone. The camera picture, the AI’s work and your motion sensors stay on your phone. Fonts and the AI model are packed with the game, so no third-party service sees your visits. Only the features listed below send anything, and most of them are off until you turn them on.

What stays on your phone

  • Motion sensors are used to draw. Their readings are not sent anywhere.
  • The camera is used only if you turn on “Draw over the camera”, to show your surroundings behind the drawing. Camera images are never saved or sent.
  • Your game progress (levels, stars, XP, trophies, streak) is saved on the phone only.
  • The AI’s voice and sounds are made on the phone. Nothing you say or hear is recorded.
  • Settings such as language and brush reach are saved on the phone.

What is sent, and when

Rankings

When you finish the daily challenge or a level, the nickname you chose, your score, the object and a random ID created for this phone are sent to the shared leaderboard. Anyone using Tilt Sketch can see nicknames and scores. Choose a nickname that does not identify you if you prefer. Offensive names are filtered, any player can report a name with the ⚑ button, and reported names are hidden.

To remove your nickname and scores from every ranking, use Settings › Your profile › Delete my rankings.

Reports

When you report a name, the name, the ranking it was on and your phone’s random ID are stored so repeated reports can hide it.

Games on several phones

In a room, the players’ nicknames and small pictures of their drawings are passed between the phones in that room through a live connection. They are not stored after the game.

Help train the AI (off by default)

If you turn it on, the lines of your drawings, the object they were meant to be, the AI’s guess and score, and a random phone ID are sent so we can improve the AI. No name, photo, camera image or location is sent. You can turn it off and delete every drawing you shared from Settings at any time.

Feedback (only when you tap Send)

Your answers, an optional comment, the language and a random phone ID.

Share with my clinician (website test feature, not in the Android app; off by default)

If you enter a clinic code, the name you type for your clinician and your practice summaries (scores, steadiness, smoothness, speed and range of motion) are sent to that clinic. Only someone with the clinic’s code and private key can read them. Drawings and other profiles are not sent. “Stop sharing” deletes that data from the clinic.

Where data is kept

Data that is sent is stored in a database hosted by Supabase, protected so the public app can only use specific, checked actions. It is not sold, rented or used for advertising. Donated drawings are used only to train and test the Tilt Sketch AI.

Children

Tilt Sketch is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Safety

Tilt Sketch is a game. Play in an open space and take a break if your wrist gets tired. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Your choices

  • Turn off drawing donations and delete the drawings you shared in Settings › Help train the AI.
  • Clear everything stored on the phone by uninstalling the app or clearing the site data.
  • Remove your nickname and scores from the rankings with Settings › Your profile › Delete my rankings.
  • For any other data, contact us below. We will delete it within 30 days.
